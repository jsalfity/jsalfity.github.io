I am a final year robotics and AI PhD student in the Nuclear and Applied Robotics Group at UT Austin, advised by Mitch Pryor. My current research focuses on robotic skill and behavior composition for modular robots. The first two years of my PhD focused on game theory, trajectory generation, and nonlinear optimization.

I co-founded geCKo Materials in 2021, a hardware manufacturing startup commercializing biomimetic industrial adhesives. We've recently raised ~$10M, built a team of 10+, and ship gMDA weekly. Our CEO and the team have been crushing it!

During my PhD, I spent summers at Cruise on the Behaviors team working on DriveGPT and on the Motion Planning & Control Analysis team working on performance metrics, and at GM working on visuomotor diffusion policies for grasping and manipulation. Before my PhD, I spent time at HP Labs in Palo Alto, CA as a Roboticist and Research Engineer, working across mobile robotics, AI/ML, 3D printing, and edge compute.

Publications

Contract-Grounded Behavior Tree Synthesis via Coding Agents
J Salfity, B Anderson, M Pryor
In submission pdf Project Webpage
A contract-grounded architecture for synthesizing deployable robot behavior trees from natural language, in which a coding agent queries a robot-side MCP server for an explicit contract of skills, operators, and composition templates before generating a validated, executable behavior tree. Non-expert operators can issue commands without knowledge of robot implementation details, while a runtime validation gate enforces correctness. Evaluated across 110 simulated PyRoboSim tasks and 14 tasks on a physical Husarion Panther robot with both closed (Sonnet 4.6) and smaller open-source (Gemma4:31b) LLMs, showing near-perfect BT validation, strong task success, and successful transfer to physical hardware running an opaque Nav2 stack.
Deployment Is Not Destiny: Robot Recomposition in the Field with Unseen Software, Hardware, and Compute Payloads
S Swanbeck*, J Salfity*, J Gunawan, C Van Sice, B Anderson, M Pryor
In submission Project Webpage
A plug-and-play framework for runtime recomposition of modular robots, enabling non-expert users to integrate unseen software, hardware, and compute payloads in the field within minutes. Demonstrated in disaster response scenarios at an operational nuclear reactor facility and in thermal-guided search.
Limited Linguistic Diversity in Embodied AI Datasets
S Wanna*, A Luhtaru*, J Salfity, R Barron, J Moore, C Matuszek, M Pryor
Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL), July 2026 paper
A systematic audit of widely used VLA corpora showing that many datasets rely on highly repetitive, template-like commands with limited structural variation. We quantify instruction language across lexical variety, duplication, semantic similarity, and syntactic complexity to support more principled dataset selection and targeted augmentation strategies.
No Reboot Required: Real-Time Modular Robot Payload Integration
J Salfity, C Van Sice, B Chen, S Swanbeck, B Anderson, M Pryor
IEEE International Conference on Ubiquitous Robotics, July 2025 paper
A runtime integration system enabling modular robots to incorporate new hardware and software payloads during live operation, eliminating the need for system reboots and enabling continuous deployment.
Temporal and Semantic Evaluation Metrics for Foundation Models in Post-Hoc Analysis of Robotic Subtasks

IEEE International Conference on Ubiquitous Robotics Workshop on Virtual Environment-Based Synthetic Data Generation for Robotic Training, July 2025 pdf

Inverse Matrix Games with Unique Quantal Response Equilibrium
Yue Yu, J Salfity, D Fridovich-Keil, U Topcu
IEEE Control Systems Letters, 2022 paper
Inverse game problems in multiplayer matrix games under perception noise, inferring cost functions such that a desired joint strategy constitutes a unique quantal response equilibrium, with algorithms based on semidefinite programming and bilevel optimization.
Development of a Robotic System for Automated Decaking of 3D-Printed Parts
H Nguyen, N Adrian, JLX Yan, J Salfity, W Allen, QC Pham
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), May 2020 paper
A robotic system using computer vision and manipulator control to automate the removal of residual powder from 3D-printed parts, enabling scalable post-processing for powder-bed fusion manufacturing.
Cost-effective Machine Learning Inference Offload for Edge Computing
C Makaya, A Iyer, J Salfity, M Athreya, MA Lewis
HP Labs Technical Report, 2020 pdf
An offloading mechanism that leverages on-premises edge compute resources to handle ML inference for resource-constrained devices, reducing reliance on distant cloud servers. Demonstrated on person and object detection for mobile robots and video surveillance with improvements in accuracy and latency.
